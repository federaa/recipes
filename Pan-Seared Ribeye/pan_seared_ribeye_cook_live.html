Pan-Seared Ribeye

Serves 2 3 cm · medium-rare Cast iron or carbon steel · high gas Pull 50 °C · land 54–56 °C

Sequence

    Mise en place

    Weigh each steak. Salt is 1 g per 100 g of that steak. The mushroom cream pepper sauce is a separate recipe. Spoon it beside the steak, or under it, once the crust has set. Do not cook the steak in it.
