# North African Mushroom & Onion Shakshuka

**Serves 4–5** (**8 eggs**). Built for the **Le Creuset 3.5 qt (3.3 L) enameled braiser** (`29.2 cm` diam × `6.4 cm` high, `5.4 kg`). Mushrooms seared dry first and lifted out so they brown instead of steaming, heavy sweet onions and peppers, cumin-led Maghrebi spice with caraway, coriander, harissa, and preserved lemon rind, and 8 eggs poached covered on low gas and pulled early so the braiser's cast-iron mass finishes the whites without chalking the yolks.

Companion file: `north_african_mushroom_shakshuka_cook_live.html`

---

## Where it comes from

Started from Cafe Mogador's *Moroccan Eggs* (spicy tomato sauce with onions, garlic, peppers/jalapeños, cumin, paprika, chili heat, a touch of honey, and eggs poached in the pan beside za'atar pita).

Departures for this kitchen: added **300 g** seared mushrooms, doubled down on onions (**450 g**) and cumin (**8 g**, split whole seeds and ground), and pushed the spice base further into North African territory with **caraway, coriander, harissa, and rinsed preserved lemon rind**, finished off-heat with **sheep's-milk feta** and fresh herbs. **Gochujang was left out deliberately**—its fermented-soy and sweet rice-syrup profile fights caraway, preserved lemon, and sheep's feta, and overlaps with harissa, tomato paste, and browned mushrooms.

## Ingredients

### Mushrooms
- Cremini (or maitake / shiitake) mushrooms, sliced 5–6 mm thick — **300 g**
- Olive oil — **20 ml**
- Fine sea salt — **2 g**

### Aromatics & spices
- Olive oil — **30 ml**
- Yellow onions (~2 medium-large), thinly sliced pole-to-pole — **450 g**
- Fine sea salt — **2 g**, *plus more at the taste gate*
- Red bell pepper (½ large), thinly sliced — **100 g**
- Fresh jalapeño, thinly sliced (seeded for moderate heat, whole for Cafe Mogador heat) — **1** (~25 g)
- Garlic (~5 cloves), thinly sliced — **25 g**
- Whole cumin seeds — **4 g**
- Ground cumin — **4 g**
- Ground coriander — **3 g**
- Caraway seeds, lightly crushed — **2 g**
- Sweet paprika (or sweet pimentón) — **4 g**
- Tomato paste — **25 g**
- Harissa paste (Tunisian or rose) — **25 g**

### Sauce
- Whole peeled plum tomatoes, hand-crushed with their juices — **1,600 g** *(2 × 800 g cans)*
- Water, to rinse the tomato cans — **60 ml**
- Preserved lemon rind, pulp scraped out, rinsed, finely minced — **15 g** *(about ½ lemon)*
- Honey — **up to 5 g**, *only at the taste gate if the tomatoes read sharp*

### Eggs & finish
- Large eggs — **8**
- Fine sea salt — **~1 g** *(a tiny pinch per egg)*
- Sheep's-milk feta, coarsely crumbled — **80 g**
- Fresh cilantro — **20 g**, flat-leaf parsley — **10 g**, fresh mint — **5 g**, roughly chopped
- Extra-virgin olive oil — **15 ml**, to finish

*(Serve with warm pita brushed with olive oil and za'atar, or crusty sourdough.)*

## Method

### 1. Sear the mushrooms
**Warm** the Le Creuset 3.5 qt braiser over medium gas with *20 ml* olive oil, then step to medium-high. **Spread** *300 g* sliced mushrooms across the floor without salt. **Cook** undisturbed for *3 minutes*, then stir occasionally until their water cooks off and the edges are deeply browned, *6–8 minutes* total. **Season** with *2 g* fine sea salt and **transfer** to a bowl.

No salt until the moisture leaves, and lift them out so they hold meaty chew instead of steaming while the onions cook. Preheat enamel gradually with oil—never blast it dry on high.

### 2. Sweat the onions
**Lower** to medium gas. **Add** *30 ml* olive oil, *450 g* sliced onions, and *2 g* fine sea salt, scraping up the mushroom fond. **Cook** *10–12 minutes*, stirring often, until soft, sweet, and golden at the edges.

Sweetening this much onion concentrates natural sugars that buffer tomato acidity later.

### 3. Soften the peppers
**Add** *100 g* sliced red bell pepper and *1* sliced jalapeño. **Cook** *5–6 minutes* until tender.

### 4. Bloom the garlic, tomato paste, and spices
**Lower** to medium-low. **Clear** a space in the center, **add** *25 g* sliced garlic, *4 g* whole cumin seeds, and *2 g* crushed caraway seeds, and **fry** *60 seconds* until fragrant. **Stir** in *25 g* tomato paste and **cook** *2 minutes* until brick-red. **Stir** in *4 g* ground cumin, *3 g* ground coriander, *4 g* sweet paprika, and *25 g* harissa for *30 seconds* until toasted and aromatic.

Medium-low is the guard here: tomato paste and ground spices scorch fast on enamel if pushed on high.

### 5. Reduce the sauce
**Pour** in *1,600 g* hand-crushed tomatoes, **rinse** the cans with *60 ml* water into the pot, and **return** the browned mushrooms with any bowl juices. **Bring** to a brisk simmer, then **reduce** uncovered over medium-low, stirring occasionally, for *15–18 minutes*. **Stir** in *15 g* minced preserved lemon rind during the last *3 minutes*.

Chop the herbs, crumble the feta, stage the 8 eggs in small cups or ramekins, and warm the pita while the sauce reduces.

### 6. Read the thickness
**Drag** a wooden spoon across the floor of the braiser.

> The trail holds clean for 2 seconds before filling in: ready for the taste gate.
> Liquid floods straight back: simmer *3–5 minutes* more uncovered, then check again.
> Sticking or catching at the edges: lower the gas and loosen with *30 ml* water.

### 7. Taste the sauce
**Taste** the sauce off a spoon, keeping in mind *80 g* salty feta is still going on top.

> Balanced and slightly shy of full salt: ready for the eggs.
> Flat or under-salted: add fine salt in *2 g* steps, tasting between each (expect *0–2 g* with salted canned tomatoes, *5–7 g* with unsalted).
> Sharp or tinny: stir in *5 g* honey, then taste again.

*Make-ahead stop:* The sauce can cool and hold overnight here. Next morning, bring the braiser back to a gentle simmer (~8 minutes) and pick up at Step 8.

### 8. Set the eggs
**Drop** the gas to **low**. **Press** *8* shallow wells with the back of a spoon (*7* in an outer ring, *1* in the center) and **slip** the *8* staged eggs in together. **Drag** the tip of a spoon gently through only the thick outer clear whites to pull a little hot sauce into them without touching the yolks. **Pinch** a tiny bit of salt onto each egg.

Staging the eggs in cups puts all eight in the pan within 30 seconds so they finish evenly. Breaking only the thick outer albumen cooks the whites roughly 2 minutes faster while keeping the yolks liquid.

### 9. Poach covered
**Cover** with the Le Creuset lid and **cook** on low gas for *4 minutes*.

### 10. Read the eggs
**Lift** the lid at *4 minutes* and look at the ring of white right around each yolk.

> Outer whites set, inner collar right around the yolk still faintly glossy and wobbly, yolks liquid: cut the gas and pull the braiser off the burner now.
> Clear runny pools across the whites: cover *1–2 minutes* more, then check again.
> Whites already opaque and firm all the way to the yolk: off the heat immediately and spoon straight into warm bowls so the cast iron does not chalk the yolks.

### 11. Finish off the heat and serve
**Leave** the braiser uncovered off the heat for *2 minutes* so the 5.4 kg cast iron finishes setting that last translucent ring of white. **Scatter** *80 g* crumbled sheep's-milk feta and the chopped cilantro, parsley, and mint across the top. **Drizzle** *15 ml* extra-virgin olive oil and **serve** straight from the braiser.

## The call

Cook it in one vessel—the **Le Creuset 3.5 qt enameled braiser**—by searing the mushrooms dry first and lifting them out, sweetening *450 g* onions, caramelizing tomato paste and blooming cumin/caraway/harissa on medium-low, reducing uncovered to a 2-second spoon trail, tasting for salt before the eggs, and pulling the *8* eggs off the burner while the inner white collar is still faintly glossy so the 5.4 kg pot's carryover finishes them cleanly.

## Red team

**Vessel geometry and thermal mass.** The Le Creuset 3.5 qt braiser (`29.2 cm` diam × `6.4 cm` high, `5.4 kg`, enameled cast iron) is built for this dish. Enameled cast iron avoids metallic reaction with acidic tomato-harissa sauce. The wide opening evaporates tomato water cleanly in *15–18 minutes*, reducing *1,600 g* tomatoes plus aromatics to roughly *1.55 L* (~2.9 cm depth)—deep enough to insulate the eggs from bottom scorch while leaving ~3.5 cm headroom under the domed lid. The load-bearing trap is its **5.4 kg** thermal mass: waiting until egg whites look 100% opaque on the burner overcooks the yolks on the way to the table. Pull early at the glossy-collar gate and let *2 minutes* uncovered off-heat finish the job.

**Mushrooms without steaming.** Dumping *300 g* raw mushrooms onto already-sweated onions floods the pan with ~240 g of water and steams both. Searing them first in *20 ml* oil with no salt until browned and lifting them out keeps them meaty and builds fond for the onions.

**Volatile flavors.** Whole cumin and caraway bloom in oil for *60 seconds*; ground cumin, coriander, paprika, and harissa cook only *30 seconds* before the tomatoes arrest the heat; preserved lemon rind enters in the final *3 minutes* of reduction; cilantro, parsley, mint, and finishing olive oil go on off-heat.

**Why no gochujang.** Gochujang brings fermented soybean (*meju*) and sweet rice-syrup stickiness that clashes with caraway, preserved lemon, and sheep's feta while duplicating heat/umami already carried by harissa, caramelized tomato paste, and browned mushrooms. Keep it for a dedicated Korean-register baked-egg dish.

**Fat.** *50 ml* olive oil builds the base (*20 ml* mushrooms, *30 ml* onions/spices), *15 ml* extra-virgin olive oil finishes off-heat, and *80 g* sheep's-milk feta plus *8* runny yolks emulsify into the spiced tomato sauce on the plate.

**Salt, stacked.** The kitchen's harissa-chicken lesson applies directly here: canned tomatoes vary dramatically in built-in sodium.

| Source | Salt | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Mushrooms | 2.0 g | Measured |
| Onions | 2.0 g | Measured |
| Harissa paste, 25 g | ~0.9 g | Asserted (~3–4% NaCl) |
| Preserved lemon rind, 15 g rinsed | ~1.4 g | Asserted (~8–10% NaCl) |
| Sheep's-milk feta, 80 g | ~2.2 g | Asserted (~2.5–3% NaCl) |
| Egg tops, 8 pinches | ~1.0 g | Asserted |
| Canned tomatoes, 2 × 800 g (if salted) | ~6.5 g | Asserted (~0.4% NaCl) |
| **Total (~2.1 kg plate) with salted cans** | **~16.0 g (~0.76%)** | **Already near target; taste gate adds 0–2 g** |
| **Total with unsalted cans** | **~9.7 g (~0.46%)** | **Taste gate adds ~5–7 g in 2 g steps** |

**Acid, stacked.** Two cans of tomatoes, *25 g* tomato paste, and *25 g* harissa already carry a full acid stack. Preserved lemon is **rind only** (pulp scraped and rinsed) so it delivers fermented citrus perfume without liquid acid; no extra lemon juice or vinegar enters the sauce. Sweetened *450 g* onions, *100 g* red pepper, *65 ml* olive oil, *80 g* feta, and *8* runny yolks buffer the sharpness, with *5 g* honey held conditional at the taste gate. *(Fallback if out of preserved lemon: grate 1 fresh lemon's zest at Step 11 and expect ~1 g more salt at Step 7.)*

**Quantity.** *8* eggs and ~1.55 L of thick sauce is **4 generous plates** (2 eggs each) or **5** beside pita/bread and sides. One pot on the gas, ~50–55 minutes start to finish (~15 minutes on the day if the sauce is made ahead through Step 7).
