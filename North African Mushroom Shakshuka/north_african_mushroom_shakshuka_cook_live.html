North African Mushroom Shakshuka

Serves 4–5 · 8 eggs Le Creuset 3.5 qt braiser (29.2 cm, 5.4 kg) One pot · gas range · ~50–55 min
Mushrooms · seared dry first, lifted out
Sauce · sweet onion, cumin, caraway, harissa, preserved lemon
Eggs & finish · pulled early, finished off-heat with feta & herbs
